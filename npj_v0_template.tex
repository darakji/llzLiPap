\section*{Abstract}
% [TODO] Unstructured, <=250 words. Draft below is ~190 words -- trim/edit
% for the final numbers once Discussion/Methods are finalized.
Solid-state Li-metal batteries require interatomic potentials accurate at
the Li/garnet electrolyte interface, a regime foundation machine-learned
interatomic potentials (MLIPs) trained on bulk-periodic data do not
reliably capture. We fine-tune a MACE foundation model on Li-metal /
Li$_7$La$_3$Zr$_2$O$_{12}$ (LLZO) interface structures labeled with
real-space DFT (DFT-FE), exploiting its native support for open (non-periodic)
boundary conditions to model genuine free surfaces without the image-interaction
and dipole-correction artifacts of plane-wave slab calculations. Training data
is grown over six active-learning iterations (it0-it6, 16$\to$108 structures)
using a coverage-driven acquisition rule operating on the pretrained model's
latent embedding space, following a data-selection strategy whose superiority
over random sampling under DFT-cost scarcity has been independently established
for pretrained-MLIP latent spaces~\citep{vargaumbrich2026}. Force error falls
monotonically across iterations on a held-out 34-structure test set (component-wise
RMSE 120.5$\to$72.0~meV/\AA, MAE 69.5$\to$44.9~meV/\AA, universal$\to$it6), and
is confirmed on an independent 7-snapshot MD trajectory set. In-plane Li and
LLZO-framework diffusion coefficients extracted from it6-driven molecular
dynamics at 1100~K agree with literature-extrapolated values to within a
factor of 2-3. [TODO: one-sentence closing claim once Discussion is final.]

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} machine-learned interatomic potentials, active
learning, MACE, solid electrolyte interface, LLZO, Li-metal battery, DFT-FE,
open boundary conditions

\section{Methods}
% ===========================================================================

\subsection{System and boundary conditions}
All structures are semi-periodic: periodic in $x$ and $y$, open
(non-periodic) in $z$. This is possible because reference labels come from
DFT-FE (real-space DFT) rather than a plane-wave code, giving a single
interface with two genuine free surfaces and no periodic-image interaction
along the surface normal -- avoiding the periodic-sandwich workaround
(second interface, image dipole or correction, constrained slab thickness)
standard in plane-wave DFT treatments of this interface.

\subsection{Active-learning acquisition}
Structures were selected from a candidate pool using a coverage-driven
($D_{99}$) criterion operating on the pretrained model's latent embedding
space (farthest-point-style diversity sampling on PCA-reduced, L2-normalized
mean-pooled 256-dimensional MACE latents). [TODO: full acquisition-rule
description matching the actual per-iteration pipeline.] This design choice
follows recent independent evidence that pretrained-MLIP latent
representations are an effective, general acquisition signal for offline
pool-based active learning under DFT-labeling-cost scarcity, consistently
outperforming random acquisition~\citep{vargaumbrich2026}.
